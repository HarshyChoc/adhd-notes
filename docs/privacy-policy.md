# Privacy Policy

ADHD Notes stores note content locally on the user's Mac and, when sync is enabled, stores synced note and Google Tasks metadata on the hosted backend so the desktop app and Google Tasks can stay consistent.

## Data We Store

- Google account email and Google subject identifier
- encrypted Google refresh token
- note title, body, due date, and Google Tasks linkage metadata
- app session tokens for the desktop app

## Why We Store It

- to sign users in with Google
- to sync notes to and from Google Tasks
- to maintain canonical note/task state and deliver live updates to the desktop app

## What We Do Not Intend To Collect

- payment data
- advertising identifiers
- unrelated Google Workspace data

## Data Retention

Hosted sync data remains stored until the account is removed or the service data is deleted by the operator.
